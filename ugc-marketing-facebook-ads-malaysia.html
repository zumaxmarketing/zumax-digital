UGC & Social Media

How UGC Marketing and Facebook Ads Help Malaysian Brands Build Trust

By Zumax Digital

People scroll past countless promotional posts every day, making it increasingly important for businesses to create social media content that feels relevant and relatable. User-generated content, commonly known as UGC, can help brands demonstrate their products through everyday experiences rather than relying solely on traditional advertising.

When combined with a well-planned Facebook marketing strategy, UGC can reach audiences who have never heard of a business. Influencer partnerships can further strengthen that visibility by introducing the brand through creators whose followers already share similar interests.

What Is UGC Marketing?

UGC marketing involves using content featuring genuine customer experiences or creator-led product demonstrations to communicate a brand's value. Examples include customer reviews, unboxing videos, tutorials and lifestyle content. Brands may also commission UGC-style videos from creators and disclose paid partnerships appropriately.

Unlike highly polished corporate advertisements, these videos often use conversational language and familiar settings. A skincare brand might show a creator incorporating a product into their morning routine, while a restaurant could share a diner's experience trying its signature dishes.

The objective is to provide useful, credible information that helps potential customers picture the product or service in their own lives.

How UGC and Influencer Marketing Work Together

UGC and influencer marketing are related, but they fulfil different roles. Influencers can introduce a business to their own audiences, while creator-produced UGC can also serve as an asset for the brand's social channels and advertising.

Turn Creator Content into Facebook Ads

Once a brand has created suitable UGC, it can test the content as part of its Facebook ad marketing strategy, provided it has obtained the necessary usage rights and approvals.

For instance, a local eyewear brand could publish a creator's try-on video organically, then promote an approved version to people interested in fashion or accessories. A separate advertisement could address questions about frame sizes, comfort or delivery.

An experienced Facebook marketing agency in Malaysia can help businesses develop different creative approaches, select audiences, manage campaign budgets and evaluate which ads generate the most useful responses.

What Types of UGC Content Should Your Brand Create?

Effective content begins with understanding what customers need to know. Useful formats include:

  • Product demonstrations that show how an item works in real situations.
  • Unboxing videos that explain packaging and what customers receive.
  • Honest testimonials that describe an actual experience.
  • Before-and-after visuals where appropriate, accurate and not misleading.
  • Tutorials answering common customer questions.
  • Lifestyle videos showing how a service or product fits into everyday routines.

Instead of requesting identical scripts from every creator, allow room for individual presentation while maintaining clear product information and brand guidelines.

Measure Engagement and Business Results

Although views and likes can reveal whether content attracts attention, they do not tell the whole story. Malaysian businesses should measure the results that relate to their campaign objectives, such as meaningful engagement, website visits, enquiry quality, purchases and cost per conversion.

Comparing different content formats helps identify which messages resonate with the audience. A review video might be effective for generating interest, while a product demonstration could be more useful for encouraging enquiries.

Brands should also review performance over time and refresh creatives when engagement or conversion performance declines.

Build a Connected Social Media Marketing Strategy

An effective campaign connects creator selection, social media content, paid promotion and the customer experience after someone clicks an advertisement. Every part should communicate the same brand message and make it easy for interested customers to take the next step.
